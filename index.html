How to remember words for speaking

  1. Learn the sentence, not the word. Every entry has an example sentence. Remember the whole phrase, because that is what you will actually say.
  2. Cover the meaning, then recall. Use “Hide meanings”, look at the Japanese, say what it means, then tap to check.
  3. Say it out loud. Tap 🔊 and repeat right after it (shadowing). Speaking trains your mouth, and reading alone does not.
  4. Star what you miss. Tap ☆ on hard words and review the ★ Favorites list more often than the rest.
  5. Learn verbs in forms. On the verbs page, practice ます, て and ない together. Most real sentences need more than the dictionary form.
  6. Turn off furigana gradually. Once a word feels familiar, hide the ふりがな and read the kanji on its own.
  7. Add your own words. Words from your day (“Add more words” at the bottom of each page) stick better than a list someone else made.

A simple daily routine

10 min · New
Learn 10 new words with their sentences. Say each sentence aloud twice.
5 min · Review
Hide meanings and test yesterday’s words. Star the ones you miss.
5 min · Speak
Make one new sentence from today’s words about your own day, and say it out loud.
Moving forward in order, N5 → N4 → N3, works well. Come back to a lower level whenever words feel shaky.
